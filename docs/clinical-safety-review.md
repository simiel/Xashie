# Hashie clinical and safeguarding review gate

This is a required human gate, not a statement that code has certified medical behavior.

Reviewers needed:

- Ghanaian clinical adviser: content evidence, emergency guidance, medicines, terminology, review dates, and expiry.
- Ghanaian safeguarding adviser: minors, abuse, coercion, exploitation, self-harm, disclosure, and referral behavior.
- Ghanaian cultural/Akan adviser: respectful language, Akan/Twi terminology, context, and non-stereotyping.
- Ghanaian legal adviser: consent, privacy, retention, professional referral, emergency wording, and applicable law.

Each adviser should record approval or required changes against a versioned policy and bilingual evaluation set. No production flag should be enabled until all required reviews are complete.
